# Customer-facing privacy text (approved by the owner)

**Wording approved by the owner on 2026-10-20 (includes the legal-name and
Discord-username watermark). Not legal advice. No retention period is invented
here: the retention wording below is still HTNQ's to set. This text still has
to be added to the published privacy notice.**

Suggested addition for the owner’s privacy notice:

> When you watch mentorship videos in HTNQ Trading Mentorship, we display your
> Discord user ID and current Discord username, and your legal name where we
> hold one, on the video player as
> a personalised watermark to discourage unauthorised sharing and to help
> investigate suspected misuse. Only you see your own watermark. If misuse is
> suspected, we can also match your Discord ID to your mentorship joining records
> (for example, the name on your valid signed NDA).
>
> The legal name shown comes from your signed NDA or staff record held by the
> mentorship bot or, if there is none, from a name our admins have recorded in
> the app's admin area. Admin-recorded names are stored on our servers, visible
> only to owners and admins, never used to decide whether you can watch, and
> every time an admin views, adds, changes or removes one it is logged (without
> the name itself). You can ask us to correct or remove it.
> Short-lived technical records of playback grants and denials may be kept so we
> can operate and secure the service; HTNQ chooses how long those records are
> retained.
>
> If you have 1:1 calls with the mentorship team, recordings of those calls may
> be stored with our video provider and shown to you in the app. Only you and
> HTNQ staff (owners, admins and mentors) can view your recordings. We store
> your Discord ID and Discord display name with each recording so staff can find
> it.
>
> Watermarking is a deterrent. It does not prevent every form of screen
> recording, guarantee that every leak can be traced, or by itself prove a legal
> breach.

## NDA signed in the app (added with the NDA requirement)

**Draft wording. Not approved by the owner and not legal advice. Add to the published privacy
notice only after the owner has approved it.**

> To keep access to the mentorship videos you must have signed our non-disclosure agreement
> (NDA). If you have not signed it in Discord you can sign it inside HTNQ Trading Mentorship by
> reading it, typing your full legal name and typing "I AGREE". We record your Discord ID, the
> name you typed, the date and time, the version of the NDA and a fingerprint of its text, and we
> keep a signed PDF copy with the mentorship bot, which also sends a copy to you in Discord. We
> use the name on your NDA as the legal name in your personal video watermark.
>
> The app only keeps a short record of when it last confirmed that you had signed (your Discord
> ID and a timestamp, no name and no document) so that a brief outage of our bot does not stop
> you watching. Owners and admins can see whether a member has signed, not the name on the NDA.
> Signing is logged (your Discord ID and the time, without your name).
